From Research Question to Open Scholarship

Follow a consistent workflow from choosing a research question to publishing a reproducible, citable project.

STEP 1Ask a QuestionChoose an SDOH topic, location, population, and outcome.
STEP 2Get the DataDownload public data and document its source and date.
STEP 3Analyze in RClean, merge, analyze, and document your workflow.
STEP 4Visualize in TableauBuild maps and interactive views that communicate your findings.
STEP 5Share & CitePublish your project through GitHub and use appropriate open repositories or sharing platforms for access, reuse, and citation.

Open Scholarship in Practice

Build research that others can find, access, understand, reproduce, and reuse.

F

Findable

Make your work discoverable through GitHub and, when appropriate, a repository record or persistent identifier.

A

Accessible

Share data, code, and visualizations through openly accessible platforms and formats.

I

Interoperable

Use standard identifiers, consistent variables, and open file formats.

R

Reusable

Document your code, data sources, methods, and project files so others can build on your work.
