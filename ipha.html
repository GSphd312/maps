From Inspection to Action: Building Connected Food Safety Systems in Local Health Departments

Ba Tung (Jackey) Tran & Brian Chen, PhD

This session explores how local health departments can turn routine food inspection data into coordinated public health action. Attendees will examine how integrated data systems, dashboard tools, academic-practice partnerships, and Community Health Worker engagement can strengthen communication, improve decision-making, and support more effective food safety operations.

Together We Thrive Against AI-Driven Health Misinformation

Tobiloba Olatokun & Brian Chen, PhD

AI is reshaping the health information environment and creating new public health risks. This session explores how public health agencies can prevent, detect early incidents of, and reduce harms from AI-generated health misinformation. Attendees will learn practical strategies for communication, surveillance, and workforce readiness. By emphasizing actionable approaches that strengthen community trust and agency response capacity, participants will gain useful real-world tools.
