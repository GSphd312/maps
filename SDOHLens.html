Translating County Health Data Into Actionable SDOH Insights For Illinois Communities

Using a purposive comparative-case design,1 we selected two metropolitan counties with comparable Hispanic representation, Black population shares, and high median household incomes. We then examined five Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) domains to identify place-based and within-county subgroup disparities through an equity lens.2

From data to action

Benchmarks show how Lake County compares. Equity gaps show who's left behind: Hispanic residents are 11.5× as likely to face a language barrier and 5.7× as likely to be uninsured. These gaps point to bilingual navigators and enrollment outreach, with cross-sector partners.
